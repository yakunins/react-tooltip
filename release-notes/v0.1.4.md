## Features

- **Autoflip inside scroll containers.** When the anchor sits in a scrollable element, the bubble now flips to stay within that element's visible area, not just the viewport.
- **Fade instead of vanish.** When the anchor is scrolled fully out of sight, the bubble fades out and fades back in when the anchor returns. Previously the browser hid it instantly.

## Changes

- The bubble also fades when its anchor scrolls out of the viewport. Previously it scrolled away together with the anchor.
- Smaller injected CSS: the bubble stylesheet went from 12.5 kB to 7.4 kB. The corner geometry now uses precomputed constants and a single `clip-path` polygon.

## Fixes

- Fixed the broken 0.1.3 package: its build output landed in `lib/src/`, so the `lib/index.js` entry was missing. 0.1.4 carries all the 0.1.3 changes listed here.
- The top-placement arrow's polygon points were listed in reverse order, making the outline self-intersecting. It rendered correctly, but only by chance.
