## Features

- **`flipBack` prop.** A flipped bubble returns to its `placement` as soon as that side has room again, instead of staying flipped until the tooltip closes. Off by default, so existing tooltips behave as before.

```tsx
<Tooltip placement="top" flipBack content="Back on top when it fits">
```

- **Arrow flip.** With `flip` (on by default), the arrow also moves to `start`, `center` or `end` when the bubble would overflow the viewport or its scroll container along the anchor's edge. The bubble glides along the edge over `animationDuration`, its arrow tip staying on the anchor. Like the flip, the shift sticks until the tooltip closes unless `flipBack` is set, and a hover-only tooltip never shifts.
